# ADR-NA-002: NKA Is Canonical Truth

Status: Proposed  
Date: 2026-08-17

## Context

Chat logs are verbose, contradictory, model-dependent, and difficult to query or compile. Uploaded and generated screenplays can also diverge.

## Decision

The versioned Narrative Knowledge Asset is the canonical story. Messages and source PDFs are evidence; compiled scripts, summaries, diagnostics, and reports are projections bound to one NKA revision.

## Consequences

Every meaningful turn must query or propose changes to the NKA. Conversation can be deleted/summarized without losing story truth. Additional schema/versioning work is accepted to gain coherence and replay.

# Vision

ConSaaS Core is a factory for products that convert heterogeneous evidence into defensible action. Each product answers one consequential domain question, preserves the evidence behind its answer, and explains it in language a person can use.

## North star

An expert team should launch a production-quality Decision Intelligence product by expressing domain knowledge—not by rebuilding acquisition, orchestration, validation, audit, reporting, deployment, and UX infrastructure. HouseWise, CareerSim, Startup Evaluator, and Narrative Architect should feel domain-native while sharing the same invisible operating system.

Core standardizes the lifecycle of a decision, not the content of the decision. It supplies a typed runtime, immutable artifacts, provenance, engine and recommendation contracts, policy-controlled releases, reporting, dashboard composition, plugin governance, testing, and product scaffolding. It does not prescribe one ontology, algorithm, model, provider, or frontend.

## Ten-product test

An abstraction belongs in Core when multiple domains need the same semantics and centralizing it improves consistency, safety, or speed. Similar-looking domain algorithms remain plugins until their invariant is proven.

## Outcomes

- Product teams spend most effort on evidence and judgment.
- Every recommendation answers what changed, why, from which evidence, under which methodology.
- Connectors, engines, reporters, and renderers evolve independently.
- Historical releases remain replayable after code, schema, or provider evolution.
- Platform upgrades cannot silently change product decisions.

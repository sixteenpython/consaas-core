# CareerSim Cognitive Blueprint

## North star

Given everything known today, which overseas-education pathway maximises the probability of
superior long-term, risk-adjusted career and financial returns for this Indian student?

## Governing comparison

Every path is compared with the counterfactual of not undertaking the overseas degree. Economic
cost includes tuition, living cost and foregone income. Outcomes use completion and employment
probability, salary ranges, India-return earnings, work rights, visa uncertainty and evidence
authority. Prestige is not an objective by itself.

The value analogue to PEG is **cost to risk-adjusted annual earnings uplift**. Lower is preferable
when quality, employability and resilience remain adequate.

## Hard gates

- all-in cost and debt must fit the family's responsible ceiling;
- fragile debt cannot be justified by an upside salary headline;
- destination work rights and programme eligibility require current verification;
- a written offer, scholarship and loan terms supersede pathway estimates;
- when no robust path clears the gates, recommend WAIT or do not invest yet.

## Consultation interpretation

Long or anxious wording is distilled into degree, field, destination, budget, funding, readiness,
career goal, objective and risk capacity. Uncertainty is valid evidence. The system may ask fewer
questions only when sensitivity testing proves that the remaining bounded answers cannot change the
verdict or leading path.

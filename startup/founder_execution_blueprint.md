# Founder Execution Assessment Blueprint

Assess only evidence contained in the eleven responses: founder–problem access, completed acts of building or selling, learning after contradictory evidence, identification of the largest unresolved risk, and a time-boxed milestone with a measurable pass/fail threshold.

Do not treat fluent English, confidence, optimism, elite credentials, long answers or declarations of passion as execution evidence. A concise answer containing a completed action and measurable result outranks a polished but unverifiable story.

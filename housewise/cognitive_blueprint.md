# HouseWise Cognitive Blueprint

## North star

Given everything known today, which property decision maximises long-term, risk-adjusted household
value without making the household financially fragile?

## Governing comparison

Every covered micro-market is processed before consultation. The model combines full acquisition
cost, financing cash flows, rent net of vacancy and maintenance, P10/P50/P90 price growth,
liquidity, liveability, transit, climate, water, supply and evidence authority.

The value analogue to PEG is **price per square foot divided by base growth plus net rental yield**.
Lower is preferable only when resilience, liquidity and property-level diligence gates hold.

## Hard gates

- a stretched household recommendation is RENT/WAIT even when the upside forecast is attractive;
- legal title, encumbrance, approvals, engineering condition and project-specific RERA evidence are
  mandatory before purchase;
- adverse water, flood, climate or unfulfilled-infrastructure evidence overrides a composite score;
- the customer receives search zones, not an unverified approval of a specific property;
- when no robust zone fits, recommend WAIT rather than force a purchase.

## Consultation interpretation

The consultant distils the city, job-to-be-done, all-in ceiling, usable size, holding period,
financing resilience, priority and risk capacity. Remaining preference questions may be skipped only
after bounded sensitivity tests establish a stable verdict and leading search zone.

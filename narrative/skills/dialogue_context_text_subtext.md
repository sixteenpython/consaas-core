# Dialogue: Context, Text and Subtext

## Purpose
Create dialogue that behaves as dramatic action rather than exposition.

## Method
Define context: who wants what, what changed just before the exchange, and what cannot be said. Define text: the playable spoken line. Define subtext: the intention, threat, concealment or emotional truth beneath it. Distinguish voices through worldview, tactics, rhythm and omission—not accents or decoration alone.

## Guardrails
Prefer behavior and implication. Remove greetings, summaries and repeated information unless they are being used as tactics.

# Scene Builder

## Purpose
Construct a scene as a mini-plot that advances the screenplay's central conflict and character arcs.

## Required scene card
Structural beat, viewpoint, entry state, immediate objective, resistance, escalation, turning point, outcome, emotional change, character behavior, dialogue context/text/subtext, blocking, and setup/payoff.

## Method
Enter late, establish a playable objective, make opposition active, escalate through behavior, turn the scene, and leave in a meaningfully different state. Connect the outcome causally to the next scene.

## Guardrails
Do not write a scene that only explains information. Do not duplicate another scene's dramatic function without deliberate escalation.

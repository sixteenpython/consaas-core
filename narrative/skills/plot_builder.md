# Plot Builder

## Purpose
Turn an author-approved centre knot, cast, archetype, genre and tone into a causal full-plot proposal.

## Method
1. Preserve every locked fact.
2. Establish the protagonist's before-state and flawed operating method.
3. Make the inciting disruption irreversible.
4. Escalate through choices and consequences, not coincidence.
5. Use the midpoint to change the meaning or cost of the goal.
6. Make the crisis destroy the old strategy.
7. Resolve external conflict through the protagonist's defining behavior.

## Output contract
Return setup, disruption, progressive complications, midpoint, crisis, climax and resolution. Mark every invention as a proposal. Do not calculate scores.

# Screenplay Structure Recommender

## Purpose
Recommend and populate one of five supported structures: Three Act, Freytag's Pyramid, Seven Point Story, Save the Cat, or Non-linear.

## Method
Judge the plot's causal density, genre promise, revelation strategy, transformation and ending. Three Act is the default. Recommend another structure only when it materially improves how the audience experiences the approved plot. Map every beat to one specific story event.

## Output contract
Return the recommendation, rationale, alternative and ordered beat events. Structure serves the story; it does not replace it.
